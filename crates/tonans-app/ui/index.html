Tonans

Microgrid Scenario Workbench

Planning estimate for human review. Tonans performs deterministic single-bus energy accounting. It is not an AC power-flow study, a protection or interconnection analysis, a safety certification, or a guarantee of savings. It never controls equipment. Results must be reviewed by a qualified engineer.
