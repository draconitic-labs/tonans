# Tonans - licence tiers and proposed pricing

**Sales are not enabled in this build.**

There is no purchase flow in Tonans, no licence key to enter, no activation server,
and no payment integration compiled into the executable. The Analyst and Commercial
tiers below are proposed terms so the entitlement boundary can be reviewed before a
sale exists. They cannot be purchased today, and this build does not try to sell
them.

Every current build runs in the Evaluation tier. It does not expire, count down, or
phone home.

## Proposed tiers

|---|---|---|---|

| **Client-facing report rights** | **No** | **No** | **Yes** |
| Updates included | - | 12 months | 12 months |

One-time means the purchased version keeps working after its update window; access
to later releases may stop. The proposed model has no subscription and no remote
kill switch.

### Client-facing report rights

This is a licence term, not a claim of technical enforcement. Evaluation and
Analyst exports are for evaluation or internal use. Commercial adds the right to
provide a Tonans report to a client as part of the licensee's own work product,
under that licensee's professional judgement and liability. It does not turn a
Tonans result into a stamped design, certification, or Flintglade endorsement.

### Paid pilots and calibration

Optional pilot or calibration work may be scoped and quoted separately. It never
implies validation, certification, or endorsement of a design, and it never makes
Tonans a control system or stamped engineering deliverable.

## Limits that no tier can exceed

Tonans produces **planning estimates for human review**. It is not an AC power-flow
study, protection or interconnection analysis, safety certification, or guarantee
of savings. No tier changes that boundary.

The engine's limits on intervals, scenarios, sensitivity cells, file sizes, and
other bounded inputs are safety constraints, not upsells. An entitlement can only
be more restrictive than the compiled-in envelope, never more permissive. In
particular, no proposed tier exceeds 32 scenarios per project or 256 sensitivity
cells per study.

## Refund policy before sales

There is currently no purchase and therefore no refund transaction. Before sales
are enabled, Flintglade must publish a specific refund window, eligibility rules,
and request method and must test the complete purchase/refund entitlement path. No
undefined or unconditional refund promise is made by this release candidate.
